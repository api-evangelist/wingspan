## API Reference
- [Retrieve Summary of All Payables](https://docs.wingspan.app/reference/get_payments-summary-payables.md): This endpoint provides a comprehensive summary of all the payables, offering insights into the current state of pending and completed payments. Ideal for clients and financial departments looking for a snapshot view of their payment obligations.
- [Retrieve Approved Payables Ready for Immediate Payroll](https://docs.wingspan.app/reference/get_payments-payroll-immediate-payable.md): Fetches all payables that have been approved and are awaiting immediate payroll processing. This provides a quick view of all payments that are due for immediate payroll execution.
- [List All Payables Associated with a Client](https://docs.wingspan.app/reference/get_payments-payable.md): Provides an list of all Payables associated with a specific client, facilitating easier management and overview of the client's financial obligations.
